# useGonche — Product Vision

## The problem

Work reaches a person from many directions (ideas, commitments, requests, reminders) and almost never arrives in an actionable shape. Task apps store it; they do not help decide **what to do, when, and how**. The result is overflowing lists, plans that break on day one, and no real picture of how much time a day actually holds.

## Who it is for

A single person managing a mix of projects, meetings and loose to-dos: freelancers, independent professionals, developers and makers. useGonche is a **personal** productivity app. It is not enterprise software, not a collaborative SaaS and not multi-tenant.

## Value proposition

> Turn the chaos of daily work into organized, executable progress.

- **Capture** anything in seconds, without having to organize it.
- **Process** it into clear, actionable items with explainable priorities.
- **Plan** the day against real capacity: availability, calendar, estimates, deadlines and dependencies.
- **Execute** with the context at hand: tasks, projects, notes and files together.
- **Reflect** and feed the lessons into the next plan.

## Differentiation

useGonche does not compete on feature count. It aims to stand out through:

1. A coherent flow from capture to execution.
2. Planning that adapts when reality diverges from the plan, without rewriting history.
3. A natural link between tasks, projects, notes and documents.
4. A manual-first experience; automation and AI are optional.
5. Help deciding what to do next, with visible reasons instead of opaque scores.
6. Centralizing work from other tools (later) without forcing the user to leave them.

Honest note: Sunsama is the closest existing product (a manual daily-planning ritual); Motion automates scheduling with opaque AI; Todoist and Notion are task and database tools. The differentiators above are hypotheses that must be proven with real use.

## Methodology: Flow Cycle

A provisional loop (not scientifically validated, open to revision): Capture -> Clarify -> Prioritize -> Plan -> Execute -> Reflect. The user sees four stages: **Capture, Process, Today, Reflect**. Clarify and Prioritize run inside Process as suggestions; Plan and Execute live in Today. Reflect feeds the next plan. Details live in [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md).

## Product principles

- The user is never forced through every stage and can work manually at any time.
- The app works fully without AI. AI only proposes and never changes data without explicit authorization.
- Do not copy features just because competitors have them; avoid metric and settings overload.
- Do not turn every productivity technique into a module.
- The user owns their data. The core domain is never coupled to external providers.
- Integrations bring work in for triage; an external item is not a task until the user converts it.
- Design for evolution (multi-user, collaboration) without implementing it before it is needed.
- No abstractions that do not pay for themselves in the current version.
